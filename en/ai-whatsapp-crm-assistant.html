What it does

AI assists the customer workflow.

  • Helps respond faster to common questions.
  • Captures lead information from conversations.
  • Updates the CRM automatically.
  • Adds conversation summaries to the lead/contact record.
  • Detects when human handoff is needed.
  • Keeps the workflow visible and auditable.
What it does not do

Humans still own the customer relationship.

  • It does not replace your sales team.
  • It does not negotiate discounts.
  • It does not approve exceptions.
  • It does not make business decisions.
  • It does not answer sensitive or high-risk questions without handoff.
  • It does not guess when the knowledge base is unclear.
