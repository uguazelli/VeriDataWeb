The Tools

Three tools. One recommendation.

Most automation problems can be solved with any of these. The right choice depends on your data, your volume, and your budget.

Make

Visual and powerful. Better than Zapier for complex branching logic. Still SaaS, but significantly cheaper and more capable. Good fit when the team wants a visual interface and data residency isn't a constraint.

Recommended

n8n

Self-hosted. Your data stays in your infrastructure. No per-task pricing — cost is flat regardless of how many automations you run. Workflows live in your repo like real code. Best fit for companies with compliance requirements or serious automation volume.

Zapier

The most recognized name. Fine for simple, low-volume workflows. If you're already on it and it works, we can optimize what you have. Not the recommendation when you're growing fast or running sensitive data through it.

AI Integration

AI is just another
integration problem.

Most companies want AI in their workflows. Very few have the plumbing to make it work reliably. Connecting an LLM to a real business system — with error handling, data validation, and something that doesn't hallucinate your customer records — is an integration job. Not a prompt engineering job.

→

LLM API integration

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or local models via Ollama connected to your existing systems. Structured outputs. Retry logic. Cost controls. Not a demo — a production integration.

→

AI agents in n8n

Autonomous workflows that reason and act: classify inbound data, draft responses, route decisions, summarize documents — triggered by real events in your systems, not by a chat window.

→

RAG pipelines

Your internal documents, policies, and knowledge bases made queryable. Built on your infrastructure. Your data doesn't leave to train someone else's model.

→

AI-powered data extraction

Invoices, contracts, emails, PDFs. Unstructured inputs turned into structured records and routed into the right system — without a human reading each one.

Honest answers.

Do I need to already have n8n installed?

No. If n8n is the right tool for your project, the setup is included in the project scope. You get a working system, not just a design.

Why n8n instead of just Zapier?

Zapier is fine for simple, low-volume workflows. The problem is the pricing model — you pay per task, so costs grow every time your business grows. n8n is self-hosted: flat cost, your data stays in your infrastructure, workflows live in your repo. For anything with compliance requirements or real volume, n8n is the better architecture.

What if I'm already on Zapier and it's working?

Then we don't touch it. If it works and the cost is acceptable, there's no reason to migrate. We can optimize what you have, add workflows, or handle the parts Zapier can't.

Is this a one-time project or ongoing?

Both options are available. Most clients start with a fixed project to solve the immediate problem. The retainer is for companies that want the automations to keep evolving — new workflows, changes, fixes — without scoping a new project every time.

How long does a project take?

Starter projects typically deliver in 5–7 days. Growth projects in 2–3 weeks depending on complexity. Timeline is always confirmed before the project starts.

Do you build AI chatbots?

Sometimes — but usually the more valuable work is upstream. A chatbot that answers questions is only as good as the pipeline behind it: where does it get the data, how is it kept current, what happens when it doesn't know. We build the full integration, not just the chat interface.

Which AI models do you work with?

OpenAI (GPT-4o), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), and local models via Ollama for clients with data residency requirements. The model choice depends on your use case, latency tolerance, and whether your data can leave your infrastructure.
