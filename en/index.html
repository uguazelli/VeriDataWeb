Why It Matters

Most integrations look fine in the demo. The difference shows in production.

Validation fails. Data drifts. Retries are missing. Nobody gets alerted. Nobody owns the fix. When a workflow touches revenue, finance, inventory, or customer handoff, the integration layer needs more than a connector.

What has to exist after go-live

Production control What it means in practice
Validation Required fields, source rules, and formats are checked before bad data moves downstream.
Retries Known temporary failures are retried automatically, with limits and visibility.
Logs Each run leaves enough context to trace source, payload, status, and failure reason.
Alerts The owner knows when a workflow breaks before the customer, finance team, or ops team finds it.
Ownership Someone is responsible for the workflow, the handoff rules, and the escalation path.
