Main Problems

Your team has become the integration layer.

L01

Systems do not talk to each other.

CRM, ERP, ecommerce, spreadsheets, email, and internal tools all work separately. People bridge the gaps manually.

L02

Odoo is surrounded by manual work.

Odoo should run the business, but sales, finance, inventory, and reporting still depend on exports and copy-paste.

L03

Automations break silently.

A workflow stops, a webhook changes, or a connector fails. The first alert is usually a customer, a missed report, or a finance problem.

Client notes

What changes when the integration layer is owned.

Representative client situations, written as field notes rather than attributed testimonials.

Brazil · Odoo + ecommerce

Before Veridata Pro, orders needed three checks.

The team was comparing ecommerce orders, Odoo invoices, and inventory sheets by hand. Ugo helped map the sync points, validation rules, and exception path. The result was a workflow the finance and operations teams could trust without rechecking every line.

Brazil · Services operation

Leads were arriving. Follow-up was getting lost.

The business had forms, WhatsApp, CRM notes, approvals, contracts, and invoices moving in separate places. Ugo helped turn the handoff into an owned workflow with clear steps. The result was less chasing, fewer missed updates, and a process the team could actually see.

Argentina · Software agency

The interface was ready, but the client systems were not.

The agency could ship the product, but the client still needed APIs, webhooks, retries, and documentation between internal tools. Ugo helped structure the integration layer behind the scenes. The result was a cleaner delivery with fewer production surprises.

Architecture Choice

The tool follows the risk.

Not every workflow needs custom engineering. Not every workflow should live in a no-code tool.

Fast automation

n8n Automation

Best for fast operational automation.

  • Lead routing
  • CRM and Odoo updates
  • Form and WhatsApp intake
  • Approval flows
  • Spreadsheet replacement
  • Email and document workflows
Critical systems

AWS / Custom Integration

Best for critical systems.

  • Workflows that must not fail silently
  • Secure APIs
  • High-volume processing
  • Retry logic and error handling
  • Auditability and monitoring
  • Long-term ownership
