Common Problems

Odoo is powerful. The integration layer decides whether it works.

L01

Odoo does not match your real process

The standard flow looks clean in the demo, but your sales, finance, inventory, and operations teams work differently.

L02

Manual data entry around Odoo

People still copy from forms, spreadsheets, emails, ecommerce, or WhatsApp into Odoo because the surrounding workflow is not connected.

L03

External systems do not sync properly

Orders, invoices, customers, inventory, payments, and status updates move late, partially, or with inconsistent fields.

L04

Reports require spreadsheet exports

If the operating rhythm depends on weekly exports and manual cleanup, Odoo is not yet serving the business properly.

L05

Teams work in separate places

Sales, finance, inventory, and operations each have their own workaround. The data never quite agrees.

L06

Previous Odoo logic is fragile

Modules or scripts exist, but nobody trusts them, documents them, or knows what will break during the next change.
