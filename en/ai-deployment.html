What production requires

The model is one part of the system.

Access

Data, tools, and permissions

Connect only the information and actions the capability needs, with clear ownership and boundaries.

Control

Evaluations and human escalation

Define what good looks like, test edge cases, and route uncertain or sensitive work to people.

Operation

Monitoring and improvement

Observe real use, track costs and outcomes, and improve the system as the business changes.

A practical boundary

AI is useful when it improves the work.

Some problems need a deterministic workflow, a better data model, a new product, or a process change. VeriData will recommend those options when they are safer, simpler, or more valuable than adding AI.
