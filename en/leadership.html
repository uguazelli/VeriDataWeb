Background

Ugo spent 15+ years inside large enterprise environments — Deloitte, Ubisoft, Accenture, Alithya — designing the integration layer that makes complex systems talk to each other.

He founded Veridata Pro to bring that same thinking to smaller companies — the ones that are still copying data between systems by hand, running Odoo beside disconnected tools, and relying on fragile workflows that break silently in production.
