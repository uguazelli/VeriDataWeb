FAQ

Common Questions

How many hours per month does this take from my team?

Very little. The vCISO retainer covers 10–15 hours of Ugo's time per month — split between async reviews, scheduled calls, and audit support. Your team's involvement is minimal: typically 1–2 hours per month for check-ins and policy approvals.

What happens if I get breached?

Your retainer includes a documented Incident Response Plan and Escalation Protocol. If something happens, there's a clear playbook: contain, investigate, notify, recover. Ugo works with you through the incident — and VeriGate's logging gives you the forensic trail to support it.

Do I need to change my code?

No. VeriGate is specifically designed to wrap existing systems without modifying your codebase. SSO, MFA, and API security are applied at the gateway layer. Your development team doesn't need to be involved in the deployment.

How fast can I be audit-ready?

Most clients can respond to vendor questionnaires within 2–3 weeks of VeriGate deployment. Full SOC 2 Type II readiness typically takes 3–6 months, but the infrastructure controls are in place from day one — which is often enough to unblock a deal.

What frameworks do you cover?

SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, NIST CSF, and LGPD (Brazil's data protection law). If your buyer uses a custom vendor questionnaire, Ugo has seen most formats across US and European enterprises and can map your controls to their requirements.

Is this a long-term commitment?

The Security Assessment and VeriGate Deployment are one-time engagements — no strings attached. The vCISO retainer runs month-to-month. Most clients stay because the value is clear, not because they're locked in.
