What forward-deployed means here

The person who understands the problem can stay close to the solution.

01Direct collaborationWork with leaders, operators, users, and technical teams instead of passing the problem between handoffs.
02End-to-end responsibilityMove from discovery and scope through architecture, implementation, deployment, and adoption.
03Production workMake the real changes—code, cloud, data, security, AI, process, or integration—required for the result.
