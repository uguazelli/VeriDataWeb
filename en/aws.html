Will this actually pay for itself?

In most cases, yes — in month one. AWS waste is almost universal in companies at your stage: dev environments running overnight, wrong storage classes, oversized instances chosen because "we might need it later", NAT Gateway costs nobody noticed. I typically find $1,500–3,000 in recoverable monthly spend in the first review. Not guaranteed — I haven't seen your account yet. But it's rare that I don't find it.

How many hours of my team's time does this take?

One hour a month for the review call, and async answering of any context questions I have. I don't need meetings to review documents. I read, analyze, and deliver results asynchronously.

What if I need something implemented, not just reviewed?

I don't write the Terraform or build the VPC for you. If you need hands-on-keyboard execution, you need a contractor. I provide the blueprints, logic, and validation so your team can build it right the first time.

Do you work with Azure or GCP too?

AWS is the primary focus. I have Azure architecture experience, but the advisory practice is built around the sharpest expertise, which is AWS and API integration workloads.

How quickly can you start?

Typically within 7 days. Because the practice is capped at a handful of clients, I only take on engagements when I have the guaranteed bandwidth to deliver.

What does the monthly review actually look like?

I review your recent configuration changes, upcoming feature plans, and current infrastructure state. You get a prioritized document specifying exactly what needs fixing immediately, what to monitor, and what's perfectly fine.
